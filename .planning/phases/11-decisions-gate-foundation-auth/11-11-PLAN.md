| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|

| T-11-49 | Information disclosure | passwords in argv, history or logs | high | mitigate | getpass/stdin only for the CLI; wizard password never echoed or audited |
| T-11-50 | Spoofing | default credentials | high | mitigate | legacy-pair scan, 401 login test, zero seed users, ruff S105/S106 |
