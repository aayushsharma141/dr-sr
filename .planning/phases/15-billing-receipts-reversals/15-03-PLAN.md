# Plan 15-03: Payments & Billing REST API with Reversal & Dues Ledger

## Purpose
Build Pydantic schemas, endpoints, and services for recording consultation fees and treatment plan instalment payments, computing pending dues dynamically, and providing Admin-only reversal with mandatory reason tracking.

## Requirements Covered
- `BIL-01`, `BIL-02`, `BIL-03`, `BIL-05`
- RBAC: `BILLING_READ`, `BILLING_WRITE`, `BILLING_REVERSE` (Admin-only).
- Audit trail: `PAYMENT_RECORDED`, `PAYMENT_REVERSED`.

## Proposed Changes

### 1. `backend/src/kalyan_api/schemas/billing.py`
- `PaymentCreate`: `patient_id`, `treatment_plan_id` (optional), `appointment_id` (optional), `amount_paise` (> 0), `payment_method` (`CASH`, `UPI`, `CARD`), `notes` (optional).
- `PaymentOut`: All payment fields, patient details, receipt number, creator username, reversal details.
- `PaymentReversalCreate`: `reversal_reason` (min_length=5, max_length=1000).
- `PendingDueOut`: `patient_id`, `patient_urn`, `patient_name`, `treatment_plan_id`, `plan_name`, `agreed_price_paise`, `paid_paise`, `balance_paise`.

### 2. `backend/src/kalyan_api/routers/billing.py`
- `POST /api/payments`: Record payment, allocate atomic receipt number, validate overpayment limit on packages, emit `PAYMENT_RECORDED`.
- `GET /api/payments/{id}`: Retrieve payment details.
- `GET /api/patients/{patient_id}/payments`: Retrieve patient payment history.
- `GET /api/billing/pending-dues`: List patients with active treatment packages having pending balances.
- `POST /api/payments/{id}/reverse`: Admin-only endpoint marking payment reversed, emitting `PAYMENT_REVERSED`. Rejects already reversed payments (400) or non-admin callers (403).

### 3. Automated Tests in `backend/tests/test_billing_api.py`
- Reception records consultation payment and package payment.
- Overpayment on package returns 422 Unprocessable Entity.
- Pending dues list reports correct balance (`agreed - paid`).
- Admin reverses payment; pending dues balance is restored.
- Non-admin attempting reversal returns 403 Forbidden.
- Audit logs verified for creation and reversal.
