### Accent Reserved For

Accent color (`#8E1C3D` primary clinic crimson, `#C39A35` kalyan gold) is strictly reserved for the following enumerable list (never "all interactive elements"):

1. Primary Workspace CTA button ("Finalise Consultation")
2. Primary Prescription CTA button ("Authorise Prescription")
3. Jump list active navigation rail indicator line and active tab border (`#8E1C3D`)
4. Active sidebar navigation indicator tab (`#C39A35`)
5. Keyboard focus visible outline rings (`focus-visible:outline-[#8E1C3D]`)
6. Severe Allergy clinical warning badge background (`#8E1C3D`)
7. Session takeover modal primary confirmation button ("Take Over Session & Sign In")

Destructive color (`#B91C1C`) is strictly reserved for:
1. "Cancel Prescription & Re-issue" action trigger and modal confirmation
2. Offline auto-save error banner ("Network error — unsaved changes present")
3. Critical vital signs alert badge (e.g. extreme BP or severe hypoxemia)

## Detailed Section Visual & Interaction Specifications

### 1. Continuous Single-Page Layout & Navigation Rail (D-19-06, D-19-07)
- **Top Sticky Bar:**
  - Left: Patient Full Name (`24px`, semibold), URN chip (`12px`, mono), Age/Gender badge, Department badge (`PHYSIOTHERAPY` or `OPHTHALMOLOGY`).
  - Center: Debounced auto-save pill (`12px` font, 3s debounce, explicit status).
  - Right: Action buttons: "Authorise Prescription" (secondary crimson outline), "Print Consultation Summary" (letterhead icon, active only if finalised), "Finalise Consultation" (primary filled crimson `#8E1C3D`).
- **Left Jump Navigation Rail:**
  - 6 anchor links with vertical progress track:
    1. Chief Complaints & History
    2. Vital Signs & Biometrics
    3. Specialty Assessment (Physiotherapy / Ophthalmology)
    4. Diagnosis & Clinical Impressions
    5. Prescriptions & Medication Orders
    6. Follow-up Recommendation
  - Active section highlighted with `#8E1C3D` left border and semibold font.
  - Smooth scroll behavior (`scroll-behavior: smooth`) on anchor click.
- **Persistent Clinical Summary Sidebar:**
  - Width: `320px` (desktop), collapsable drawer on tablet.
  - Patient Card: Demographic details, photo thumbnail, emergency contact.
  - Allergy Alert Banner: High-contrast crimson card (`#8E1C3D` background, `#FFFFFF` text) when allergies exist; muted slate card when "None reported".
  - Previous Encounter Card: Displays summary of the patient's most recent prior visit (Date, Doctor, Diagnosis, Chief Complaint). Contains a "Copy Section Findings" button for each section allowing doctors to pull prior text into the active draft with a single click. **Nothing is auto-copied without explicit doctor initiation.**

### 2. Vitals & Biometrics Section (DWS-06)
- **Grid Layout:** 4-column responsive grid (`gap: 16px`).
- **Inputs:**
  - Pulse (bpm): Number input, range 30–260.
  - Blood Pressure (mmHg): Two inputs separated by `/` (Systolic: 40–300, Diastolic: 20–200).
  - SpO2 (%): Number input, range 40–100, trailing `%` symbol.
  - Body Temperature (°F): Number input, range 85.0–110.0, step 0.1.
  - Height (cm): Number input, range 20–280.
  - Weight (kg): Number input, range 1–400, step 0.1.
  - Respiratory Rate (/min): Number input, range 5–80.
- **Live BMI Badge:**
  - Calculated automatically whenever Height and Weight are both valid: `BMI = Weight / (Height / 100)^2`.
  - Visual Pill: Displays numeric value (e.g. `22.4 kg/m²`) + Classification:
    - `< 18.5`: Underweight (Slate blue pill)
    - `18.5 – 24.9`: Normal Weight (Emerald green pill)
    - `25.0 – 29.9`: Overweight (Amber orange pill)
    - `>= 30.0`: Obese (Crimson red pill)

### 3. Specialty Clinical Assessment Variants (D-19-10, D-19-11, D-19-12)
- Form dynamically loads based on appointment's department:
- **Variant A: Physiotherapy (`PHYSIOTHERAPY_V1`)**
  - **Pain Assessment:**
    - Interactive VAS Slider: 0 to 10 scale with numeric indicator and 5 color-coded milestone labels:
      - `0`: No Pain (Green)
      - `1–3`: Mild Pain (Emerald)
      - `4–6`: Moderate Pain (Amber)
      - `7–9`: Severe Pain (Orange)
      - `10`: Worst Imaginable Pain (Crimson)
    - Anatomical Site: Text input / joint selector.
    - Pain Characteristics: Multi-select pill group (Sharp, Dull Ache, Throbbing, Burning, Stabbing, Tingling).
    - Aggravating & Relieving Factors: Text areas.
  - **Range of Motion (ROM):**
    - Joint inspected dropdown (Cervical Spine, Lumbar Spine, Shoulder, Elbow, Wrist, Hip, Knee, Ankle).
    - Movement rows (Flexion, Extension, Abduction, Adduction, Internal Rotation, External Rotation).
    - Status selector per movement: Normal, Mildly Restricted, Severely Restricted, with optional degrees input.
  - **Strength & Function:**
    - Muscle Group inspection table with MMT Grade selector buttons (0/5, 1/5, 2/5, 3/5, 4/5, 5/5).
    - Ambulation status radio group: Independent, Assisted (with assistive device), Non-ambulatory.
  - **Posture & Gait:**
    - Posture Alignment: Normal, Kyphotic, Lordotic, Scoliotic, Forward Head, Pelvic Tilt.
    - Gait Analysis: Normal, Antalgic, Trendelenburg, Ataxic, Hemiplegic, Shuffling.
    - Assistive Device Used: None, Walking Cane, Walker, Crutches, Wheelchair.
- **Variant B: Ophthalmology (`OPHTHALMOLOGY_V1`)**
  - **Side-by-side Dual Column Layout:**
    - Left Column: **Right Eye (OD)** (distinct blue-tinted subtle border).
    - Right Column: **Left Eye (OS)** (distinct green-tinted subtle border).
  - **Visual Acuity Panel:**
    - Distance Vision Uncorrected: Pick-list (6/60, 6/36, 6/24, 6/18, 6/12, 6/9, 6/6).
    - Pinhole Vision: Pick-list.
    - Best Corrected Visual Acuity (BCVA): Pick-list.
    - Near Vision: Pick-list (N6, N8, N10, N12, N18, N24, N36).
  - **Intraocular Pressure (IOP):**
    - OD value (mmHg) & OS value (mmHg).
    - Measurement Method: Non-contact Tonometry, Goldmann Applanation Tonometry.
  - **Slit Lamp Biomicroscopy (Anterior Segment):**
    - Lids & Adnexa (text input)
    - Conjunctiva (text input)
    - Cornea (text input)
    - Anterior Chamber (Depth: Deep/Normal/Shallow; Cells/Flare: None/1+/2+/3+/4+)
    - Iris & Pupil (Pupil: Round Regular Reacting [RRR], Sluggish, Dilated)
    - Lens status (Clear, Nuclear Sclerosis [Grades 1–4], Cortical Cataract, Posterior Subcapsular, Pseudophakic [PCIOL])
  - **Fundus & Posterior Segment:**
    - Optic Disc: Cup-to-Disc (C:D) ratio (e.g. 0.3, 0.5, 0.7), Margins (Distinct/Blurred).
    - Macula: Normal foveal reflex, Dull reflex, Edema, Drusen.
    - Retinal Vessels: Normal caliber, Attenuated, AV crossing changes.
  - **Refraction:**
    - Grid with Sphere, Cylinder, Axis, Add for OD and OS.

### 4. Prescription Authorisation & Lifecycle (DWS-07)
- **Integrated Prescription Table:**
  - Columns: `#`, `Medicine Name`, `Strength`, `Form`, `Route`, `Dosage`, `Frequency`, `Duration (Days)`, `Special Instructions`, `Actions`.
  - Form dropdown: Tablet, Capsule, Eye Drops, Eye Ointment, Syrup, Gel, Injection.
  - Route dropdown: Oral, Right Eye (OD), Left Eye (OS), Both Eyes (OU), Topical, Inhalation.
  - Frequency dropdown: OD (Once daily), BD (Twice daily), TDS (Thrice daily), QID (4 times daily), SOS (As needed), HS (At bedtime).
- **Status Indicator & Actions:**
  - Status Badge:
    - `DRAFT`: Gray badge. Table is editable. Action: "Authorise Prescription".
    - `AUTHORISED`: Green badge. Table is locked. Actions: "Print Prescription Slip", "Cancel Prescription & Re-issue".
    - `CANCELLED`: Red strikethrough badge. Displays cancellation reason, author, and timestamp.
  - "Authorise Prescription" Button: Validates at least 1 medicine row with name, dosage, frequency, days. Once authorised, updates status and enables "Print Prescription Slip".
  - "Cancel Prescription & Re-issue" Button: Opens modal requiring cancellation reason (>= 3 chars). On submission, marks current prescription `CANCELLED`, opens a fresh blank draft editor for reissuing, ensuring at most one active prescription per visit.

### 5. Follow-Up Recommendations (DWS-08)
- **Fields:**
  - Recommended Review Date: Native date picker (`min = today`).
  - Recommended Doctor: Dropdown of active hospital doctors (defaults to current doctor).
  - Clinical Justification Notes (Doctor Only): Multiline textarea for internal medical rationale. Strictly omitted from reception views.
  - Administrative Booking Note: Single-line input for front-desk scheduling cues (e.g. "Review with blood report after 7 days").
- **Status Flow:**
  - Defaults to `PENDING`.
  - Converted to `BOOKED` when reception or doctor schedules an appointment from the recommendation.
  - Closed with `CLOSED` status if cancelled with a reason.

### 6. Single Concurrent Session Takeover Modal (DWS-10)
- **Trigger:** HTTP 409 response with error code `session_in_use` upon password verification at `/login`.
- **Dialog Appearance:**
  - Backdrop: Native `<dialog>` modal with high-contrast crimson top accent bar.
  - Title: "Active Session Detected Elsewhere".
  - Body:
    - Alert message: "This doctor account is currently signed in on another workstation."
    - Active Session Metadata Box:
      - Workstation IP: `192.168.1.104`
      - Client Browser: `Chrome / Windows`
      - Last Active Time: `Today at 10:14 AM`
    - Safety notice: "Only one active workstation login is allowed per account. Taking over will terminate that session immediately."
  - Action Buttons:
    - Primary CTA: "Take Over Session & Sign In" (`#8E1C3D` background, white text). Submits login payload with `takeover: true`.
    - Secondary CTA: "Cancel Sign-In Attempt" (neutral border, dark text). Clears inputs and dismisses modal.

### 7. Dual Print Layout Specifications (DWS-09)
- **Document 1: Official Prescription Slip (`PrescriptionPrint.tsx`)**
  - Enabled when: `prescription.status === 'AUTHORISED'`.
  - Format: A4 Single Page (`@page { size: A4; margin: 6mm; }`).
  - Contents:
    - Top: Sri Vashishtha Kalyan Trust letterhead crest and bilingual hospital header.
    - Patient Strip: Name, URN, Age/Gender, Date, Token.
    - Diagnosis line.
    - Medication Table: Medicine, Strength, Form, Route, Dosage, Frequency, Days, Instructions. Bilingual Hindi labels (`दवा का नाम`, `खुराक`, `सेवन विधि`).
    - Bottom: Doctor Name, Qualifications, Registration No., Doctor Signature Seal Box.
- **Document 2: Patient Consultation Summary (`ConsultationSummaryPrint.tsx`)**
  - Enabled when: `encounter.is_finalised === true`.
  - Format: A4 Single Page on approved hospital letterhead.
  - Contents:
    - Top: Standard hospital letterhead and patient profile bar.
    - Section 1: Chief Complaints & Relevant Clinical Findings (plain-text summary).
    - Section 2: Clinical Diagnosis.
    - Section 3: Care, Activity & Ergonomic Advice (exercise advice, dietary tips, precautions).
    - Section 4: Recommended Follow-up Review Date and Doctor.
    - **Medication Exclusion Rule**: Strictly **NO MEDICINES LISTED** on this summary. A clear footer note states: *"For prescribed medications, refer exclusively to the authorised Kalyan Hospital Prescription Slip."*
    - Reprint Tracking: If printed after the original consultation date, displays a subtle footer stamp: `Reprinted on DD/MM/YYYY at HH:MM`.
