# Plan 14-03: Treatment Plans & Packages REST API

## Purpose
Implement Pydantic schemas, service layer, and FastAPI router for physiotherapy treatment plans and packages, with optimistic concurrency, status lifecycle, and client registration form gap reconciliation (`TRT-01`, `OQ-13`).

## Requirements Covered
- `TRT-01`: Treatment plan/package creation with agreed session count and package fee in paise.
- `OQ-13`: Registration form reconciliation for condition, referral source, session counts, and package fees.

## Proposed Changes

### 1. `backend/src/kalyan_api/schemas/treatment_plans.py`
- `TreatmentPlanCreate`: `patient_id`, `doctor_id`, `encounter_id`, `plan_name`, `condition_diagnosis`, `referred_by`, `total_sessions`, `agreed_price_paise`.
- `TreatmentPlanUpdate`: `version_id`, `plan_name`, `condition_diagnosis`, `referred_by`, `status`, `cancellation_reason`.
- `TreatmentPlanOut`: full plan attributes with `completed_sessions` and timestamps.

### 2. `backend/src/kalyan_api/routers/treatment_plans.py`
Endpoints:
- `POST /api/treatment-plans`: Doctor creates plan (`TREATMENT_PLAN_WRITE`).
- `GET /api/treatment-plans/{id}`: View plan details and progress (`TREATMENT_PLAN_READ`).
- `GET /api/patients/{id}/treatment-plans`: List plans for patient (`TREATMENT_PLAN_READ`).
- `PATCH /api/treatment-plans/{id}`: Modify or cancel plan with `version_id` optimistic concurrency check (`409 Conflict`) and mandatory cancellation reason.

### 3. Permissions & Audit
- Permissions: `TREATMENT_PLAN_READ` (Admin, Reception, Doctor), `TREATMENT_PLAN_WRITE` (Doctor only).
- Audit events: `treatment_plan.created`, `treatment_plan.updated`, `treatment_plan.cancelled`.

## Verification
- Automated tests: `backend/tests/test_treatment_plans_api.py`
  - Validates total_sessions between 1 and 200, non-negative agreed_price_paise.
  - Validates 409 conflict on stale version_id.
  - Validates cancellation requires cancellation_reason >= 3 chars.
