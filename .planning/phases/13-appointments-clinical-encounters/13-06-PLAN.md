# Plan 13-06: Frontend Doctor Consultation Queue & Clinical Encounter Workspace

## Purpose
Build doctor dashboard with live consultation queue, clinical encounter documentation workspace, finalisation locking, and dated addenda management.

## Requirements Covered
- `APT-02`: Doctor sees their day's queue in order.
- `CLN-01`: Doctor records an encounter with clinical notes for a patient.
- `CLN-02`: Doctor finalises note (locking it against edit) and records dated addenda.

## Proposed Changes

### 1. API Client Additions (`frontend/src/api/client.ts` & `queries.ts`)
- `useDoctorQueue()`: queries `GET /api/doctor/queue`.
- `usePatientEncounters(patientId)`: queries `GET /api/patients/{id}/encounters`.
- `useEncounter(id)`: queries `GET /api/encounters/{id}`.
- `useCreateEncounter()`: mutation calling `POST /api/encounters`.
- `useFinaliseEncounter()`: mutation calling `POST /api/encounters/{id}/finalise`.
- `useAddAddendum()`: mutation calling `POST /api/encounters/{id}/addenda`.

### 2. Frontend Pages & Components
- `frontend/src/pages/DoctorDashboard.tsx` (`/doctor`):
  - Surfaces doctor's today queue segmented into:
    - Currently in consultation (prominent active card).
    - Waiting room (Arrived patients sorted by token #).
    - Scheduled (Booked patients).
    - Completed today (Done).
  - One-click "Start Consultation" button for arrived patients.
- `frontend/src/pages/ClinicalEncounterWorkspace.tsx` (`/doctor/encounters/new` & `/doctor/encounters/:id`):
  - Patient header banner: URN, Full Name, Age/Gender, Mobile, Allergies (highlighted alert).
  - Historical Encounters Accordion (past consultation records for this patient).
  - Clinical Form:
    - Symptoms / Chief Complaints (Textarea).
    - Clinical Examination & Progress Notes (Textarea).
    - Diagnosis / Impression (Textarea).
  - Action buttons:
    - "Save Draft" (enabled while `is_finalised == false`).
    - "Finalise Consultation" with confirmation modal ("Once finalised, notes cannot be edited; only addenda may be added").
  - Finalised State Display:
    - Green locked badge: "Note Finalised on [Date Time]".
    - Fields rendered as read-only clinical record.
    - "Add Addendum" section: form with text area and "Append Addendum" button.
    - Chronological Addenda timeline displayed directly below notes with author and timestamp.

### 3. Tests
- `frontend/src/pages/__tests__/doctor-workspace.test.tsx`:
  - Renders today's queue in order.
  - Renders clinical encounter form.
  - Triggers finalisation and verifies locked view with addenda section.
